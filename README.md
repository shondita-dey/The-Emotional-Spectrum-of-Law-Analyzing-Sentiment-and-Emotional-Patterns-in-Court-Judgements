# The-Emotional-Spectrum-of-Law-Analyzing-Sentiment-and-Emotional-Patterns-in-Court-Judgements


By applying Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Explainable AI (XAI) techniques, this project aims to uncover hidden patterns in the tone of judicial texts and explore their potential connection with sentencing outcomes.


The project involves several stages:-


• **Data Processing & Labelling**: Preprocessing large volumes of court judgements and preparing them for sentiment and emotion classification

• **Model Development**: Training transformer-based models to classify judgements into positive, neutral or negative sentiment categories and to detect emotions such as anger, joy, sadness and fear

• **Correlation Analysis**: Investigating statistical links between the sentiment/emotion of a judgment and the severity of the sentence

• **Visualization**: Presenting results through bar plots, boxplots, heatmaps and word clouds to make patterns in judicial tone easily interpretable

• **Explainability**: Applying LIME to ensure transparency by identifying key words and phrases that drive the model’s predictions
